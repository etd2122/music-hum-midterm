Exam format

Part I · Composers & Titles ID

Hear a 30-second excerpt from the playlist → name the composer and title. Each example has a follow-up question: either an underlined term from the slides, or a musical element in that excerpt (e.g. “What is the texture?”).

Part II · Document ID

Read a short excerpt → identify it by its author/title label, then briefly explain its context and/or importance to the history we’ve studied.

Recordings matter. The exam uses the exact recordings on the professor’s Spotify/YouTube playlists. The “Lecture clip” links below come straight from the slides and may be different performances — do your final run-throughs on the playlist.
